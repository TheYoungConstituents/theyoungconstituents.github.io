Event Details

Aurora Community Impact Walk.

Aurora Community Arboretum, Aurora, ON · August 29 2026 · 11:00 AM to 12:15 PM

Back to Events & Programs

Aurora, ON · Saturday, August 29 2026 · 11:00 AM–12:15 PM

Aurora Community Arboretum · View on Google Maps

Event Purpose

Main Purpose

The purpose is to bring young people together to make a positive impact in Aurora by improving the community through a cleanup, discussing local issues affecting youth, building connections among members, planning future community initiatives, and encouraging civic participation and leadership. Ultimately, it is about turning discussion into real-world community action.

Event Plan

Key Activities

  • Community Walk — Explore the Aurora Community Arboretum together.
  • Litter Cleanup — Help keep the area clean and welcoming.
  • Youth Discussion — Discuss local issues affecting young people.
  • Initiative Planning — Brainstorm future community projects.
  • Member Networking — Meet and connect with other chapter members.
  • Social Media Content — Capture photos and highlight the chapter’s community involvement.
